**Guild Raid** is the fifth Combat Content pack and sixth overall content pack. Although it is not accesssed from the pack collection, it is still considered a content pack in the game data.

## About
:''Following a request from the Brwynn Mercenary Guild Receptionist, the group heads to a forward base on the outskirts of the city. There, they encounter an enormously massive war machine, Lancelot, and the guild receptionist Liza controlling it. Liza announces the impending appearance of a powerful and legendary monster, a 'Ruler' level beast that threatens the world and states that defeating this monster will require the cooperation of all guilds and the help of the group. Join forces with the mercenary guild and feat the 'Ruler' level monster with Lancelot!

This is a PvE (Player vs. Environment) pack where a Guild works together to battle against a raid boss while utilizing Lancelot.

This pack was released on September 12, 2024 with trial seasons, and the first official season was launched on September 25, 2025.

## Overview

A Guild Raid is started every 4 weeks. The raid consists of 7 days of Boss Defense. In this battle, players can fight alongside Lancelot against the raid boss. There are a total of 10 boss levels, and any of the levels can be attempted any number of times. The higher the level and the faster the level is cleared, the higher the score. Only the player's highest scored clear will be recorded.

Players can borrow up to 2 characters from guildmates to use in the battle, although more points are granted the fewer characters a player borrows. Characters can be borrowed up to 3 times per day, with one use being deducted after each victory. Collection buffs of borrowed characters applied based on the person borrowing them. The person who registered the support characters receives rewards based on the number of times their character was borrowed.

Differing from normal battles, the battles in Guild Raids take place on a 5x5 grid instead of the typical 3x4, and players can bring up to 7 characters into battle. Additionally, maximum {{kw|SP}} is increased to 30 from the typical 20.

Guild Raid attempts conclude 9 hours before daily reset time on the final day of the raid.

### Lancelot
In battle, a percentage gauge is filled by damaging the boss. Specifically, the gauge fills based on the player's {{kw|Chain}} count and the boss's missing HP. Upon reaching 100% of the gauge, the player has the option to let Lancelot enter the battle for 6 turns. Lancelot can be summoned only once per battle.

Lancelot is a physical type neutral property character with 15,000,000 HP and 12,000 ATK. All of its other stats are 0.

### Boss
* In the battle, the player can use Lancelot or trigger conditional effects to apply a {{kw|Break}} state on the boss.
* If the boss is in Break state, all of its reserved skills are canceled and it cannot do any actions for a certain amount of turns.
* When the boss reaches 50% HP, it enters Overdrive state, granting itself a new arsenal of regular and conditional skills.
* Attacks from the boss cannot be evaded.

### Ranking and rewards
In addition to guilds being ranked against each other, individual members within a guild are also ranked with other members. In the case that multiple members have the same score, an overkill score system is used as a tiebreaker. Overkill score is based on the additional damage a player deals to the boss on the finishing blow. When comparing guilds, overkill score is also used in the way that if two guild rankings are tied, the overkill score of the three members in the guild who dealt the most damage are added up to solve the tiebreaker.

When the raid season ends, statues are erected in the Brwynn Mercenary Guild, which can be found in Mist Man, the 3rd story pack. Each statue features the top 3 guilds and lists the three characters with the highest contribution rankings, along with their nicknames. The statues can be interacted with to view detailed information about each ranked user.

Individual players can earn Mercenary Alliance Deeds for clearing levels of the raid, at 600 Deeds per level cleared, up to 6000 total if the player clears the highest level. If the Guild earns a certain amount of points, all participating members earn additional rewards. Additionally, all members receive a reward based on how high the guild ranked for the raid.

## History
### March 25th 2026 Maintenance

Invasion Defense was an additional mode in the Guild Raid which was removed in the [March 25th 2026 Maintenance](链接). In Invasion Defense, players could fight in smaller battles of various difficulites to power up Lancelot. Invasion Defense battles were comparable to typical general battles where the player battled against multiple enemies.

Instead of the Boss Defense lasting for 7 days, Guild Raids were instead split into the Invasion Defense period which lasted for days 1-3, and the Boss Defense which lasted for days 4-7.

Depending on how the guild performed in the Invasion Defense battles, they could level up their Lancelot up to level 5, making it stronger. After Invasion Defenses were removed, Lancelot now starts at level 5 automatically for all guild raids.

Additionally in this maintenance, Lancelot's gauge was changed from being powered up by both receiving and dealing damage to only being powered up by dealing damage.
