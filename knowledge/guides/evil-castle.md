**Evil Castle** is the second Combat Content pack and third overall content pack.

## About

:''It is unknown since when, but humans have been calling these towers the Evil Castle. They weren't wrong. Demons do exist in this place, and it sure does look like a castle. Anyway, the important thing is this. Those who manage to breakthrough the countless dangers of the Evil Castle, will be handed with endless wealth and fame! Think you're strong enough? Well, what are you waiting for? Haha!

This is a PvE (Player vs. Environment) pack that is further split into 5 game modes, the towers of Pride, Desire, Jealousy, Wrath, and Salvation. The main goal of all towers is to climb floors, fighting challenging battles in the process. Evil Castle is unlocked after the player clears the 4th story pack, Cat Daddy.

This pack was released on June 22, 2023, alongside the game's orginal launch. Originally, Evil Castle only had the Tower of Pride. The mode has since been expanded further:
* On October 26, 2023, Tower of Desire was added.
* On April 11, 2024, Tower of Jealousy and Wrath were added.
* On July 18, 2024, Tower of Salvation was added.

## Tower of Pride
Tower of Pride is a mode with 6 floors. Each floor has 5 battles that must be completed. Based on the score the player has obtained in each of the floors, they get better daily rewards.

Characters cannot be changed once the player has entered a floor. All battles must be completed using the same formation.

The following affect the score the player receives from a battle:

* The amount of damage dealt on the first turn
* The amount of turns it took to finish the battle
* The remaining health of the player's characters

### Rewards
In order to receive the maximum amount of rewards from the Tower of Pride daily, the following need to be achieved:

* The player needs to deal 1 million damage in a single hit with one character in every battle
* Every battle needs to be cleared in 1 turn
* All of the player's characters need to be at full HP
* A certain amount of characters from specific properties need to be used on the different floors:
** Floor 1: 4 Fire characters must be used
** Floor 2: 4 Light characters must be used
** Floor 3: 4 Water characters must be used
** Floor 4: 4 Light characters must be used
** Floor 5: 4 Wind characters must be used
** Floor 6: 2 Light and 2 Darkness characters must be used

The player automatically receives daily Devil Coins and Ancient Crystals based on their score in the Tower of Pride. The floors do not need to be cleared daily to receive the reward. The reward is given at daily reset time every day.

If the player hasn't reached the max score and updates their high score, they will gain increased rewards starting from the next day.

## Tower of Desire
Tower of Desire is a one-time challenge content with 250 floors. Each floor contains 1 battle, and after clearing it, the player can proceed to the next floor. Each floor cleared grants rewards, with increased rewards at every 5th floor. The higher the player climbs, the harder the floors get. Every 10 floors the property of the enemies changes, and will stay as the same property for the next 10 floors.

Three challenge assignments are given on each floor, and a reward is given for each challenge completed. Each challenge can only be achieved once, and the player can re-enter a previous cleared floor to take on the challenges again if they didn't achieve some of them. The challenges are as follows:

* Challenge 1: Win the battle
* Challenge 2:
** Win the battle with less than 2 fatigued (every 5th floor)
** Win the battle with everyone survived (all other floors)
* Challenge 3: Win the battle within 8 turns or less

### Rewards
Rewards for Tower of Desire are granted only once for every floor cleared, depending on the challenges completed. The rewards are as follows:

## Tower of Jealousy and Wrath
The Towers of Jealousy and Wrath are similar towers. Like the Tower of Desire, the Towers of Jealousy and Wrath are one-time challenge content with 100 floors each. The difference is the characters that can be used: In Tower of Wrath, enemies are immune to physical damage, which means magical characters must be used to deal damage. Likewise, in the Tower of Jealousy, enemies are immune to magical damage, which means physical characters must be used to deal damage. Additional effects are added to enemies as the player climbs higher in the tower.

Unique to these towers, the enemies have the neutral property, which means they don't resist or take increased damage from any property, and they don't deal increased damage to characters of any property.

Like the Tower of Desrie, each floor contains 1 battle, and after clearing it, the player can proceed to the next floor. Each floor cleared grants rewards, with increased rewards at every 5th floor. The higher the player climbs, the harder the floors get.

Three challenge assignments are given on each floor, and a reward is given for each challenge completed. Each challenge can only be achieved once, and the player can re-enter a previous cleared floor to take on the challenges again if they didn't achieve some of them. The challenges are identical to the Tower of Desire:

* Challenge 1: Win the battle
* Challenge 2:
** Win the battle with less than 2 fatigued (every 5th floor)
** Win the battle with everyone survived (all other floors)
* Challenge 3: Win the battle within 8 turns or less

## Tower of Salvation

Tower of Salvation is the most complex of the towers and is a {{Wp|roguelike}} mode where the player climbs up floors, starting with random characters and recruiting more as they climb higher. The player collects Artifacts to give bonuses, encounters enemies, bosses, and events with random outcomes, and collects silver to purchase costumes or Artifacts in shops along the way.

Tower of Salvation uses Keys of Salvation as a currency needed to start a level. Every day at daily reset time, the player is granted up to 10 Keys of Salvation automatically. These cannot be banked, so if the player doesn't use all 10 in a day, they will only be refreshed back up to the maximum of 10 the next day. Keys of Salvation can additionally be bought from the Charge store for 1 key per 10 Dia.

Tower of Salvation only uses 5-star costumes. When the player enters the Tower of Salvation, they are prompted to choose one of 4 costumes, 4 times. The 5th costume can be freely chosen from any 5-star costume in the game, even ones the player doesn't own. The player must then use these 5 costumes to clear battles in the tower and recruit more costumes along the way. Costumes start at the upgrade level that the player owns. For example, if a player owns Liberta/Dark Saintess at +3, then that costume will start at +3 for the run.

For general character and costume growth elements, they will mirror whatever the player owns. For example, if a player has awakened a character, that character will also be awakened in Tower of Salvation. Likewise, costume potential liberation and burst can be used if they've been unlocked. The only growth element that is not carried over is character level. In Tower of Salvation, all characters are level 100 no matter what level the player has them at.

When the player clears battles in the tower, they gain a currency called Lost Silver. Silver can then be used in shops to buy useful items. Silver is only accumulated for that run, and does not carry over between different runs. Additionally, every time the player goes to a higher floor, they gain extra silver equal to 5% of their current silver total.

Another currency used in Tower of Salvation is called Night World Obsidian. When the player fully completes a level (or retreats/abandons it mid-way), they gain Obsidian. Obsidian can then be used to upgrade the player's starting growth to make it easier to clear higher levels. After a player clears a level for the first time in a season, they can quick battle that level to instantly gain the Obsidian rewards that are granted from completing that level. This way, the player can quick battle a lower level for multiple days to upgrade their stats before taking on a higher level. Higher levels grant more Obsidian.

If the player abandons their run, they will only receive 30% of the Obsidian they gathered. Retreating allows the player to keep 100% of the Obsidian gathered, but retreating cannot be done mid-battle. Retreating can only be done after succesfully clearing a Boss Battle floor and before entering the next floor.

Finally, Tower of Salvation has passive effects called Artifacts which grant useful effects to the player's characters. These can range anywhere from increasing ATK or Crit DMG, to granting {{kw|Energy Guard||Energy Guards}}, to increasing {{kw|Chain}} damage, among many other effects. Artifacts are obtained throughout the run, and some artifacts can be combined to make better ones. The full list of Artifacts can be seen at #List of Artifacts below.

### Floor types

;Normal Battle

In normal battles, the player fights against regular enemies. After winning, an amount of silver is granted and the player can choose one of 4 new costumes to add to their roster. From those 4 costumes, 2 of them will be of characters that the player already has in their roster, and 2 will be costumes of characters that the player doesn't have.

;Elite Battle

In elite battles, the player fights against stronger enemies. After winning, an amount of silver is granted and the player can choose one of 4 Artifacts. The Artifacts that can be chosen from get better as the player reaches higher levels.

;Boss Battle

In boss battles, the player fights against a single strong enemy, or a strong enemy coupled with regular enemies. After winning, an amount of silver is granted and the player can choose one of 4 Artifacts. The Artifacts that can be chosen are better than the ones granted from elite battles. Bosses are always neutral in property.

;Artifact

In Artifact floors, the player can immediately choose one of 4 Artifacts after entering that floor. The Artifacts that can be chosen from get better in higher floors. UR grade Artifacts can also be found in these floors. 

;Event

In event floors, different randomized events can happen. These can range from gaining silver, entering fights, exchanging Artifacts for other Artifacts, buying Artifacts with silver, choosing an Artifact for free, and so on. The events that can be encountered are completely random, but the player can use Artifacts to increase their odds of success in these floors.

;Shop

In shops, the player can spend their silver to purchase Artifacts or costumes, buy extra copies of the costumes in their roster, or fully heal and revive their roster. The player can upgrade their roster's costume duplicates at a price of 1000 silver per extra duplicate. The shop can also be refreshed to show new options, but the price to refresh the shop increases each time. The shop has a chance to discount any costume or Artifact it offers by 50%. This probability can also be increased by growth upgrades and Artifacts.

It should be noted that only costume duplicates can be brought from shops. Other character growth elements, such as potential liberation or burst, cannot be purchased from the shop.

;Crystal Battle

The crystal battle is a final special room that is entered after the player clears the 30th floor of level 10. In the crystal battle floor, the player gets 5 attacking turns to deal as much damage as possible to the crystal ball using the roster they've gathered. When the turns are over, the crystal is destroyed and the battle ends. The accumulated damage is saved as the player's high score and is used for ranking on the leaderboards.

### Starting growth

Before entering a level, the player can use their Night World Obsidian to upgrade their "Blessing of the Night World", this being stats and other useful effects. Each element can be upgraded up to 5 times, with the upgrades costing 100 Obsidian for the first upgrade, then 500, then 1000, then 5000, and 10,000 for the final upgrade.

The elements that can be upgraded are as follows:

* Max HP: +20/40/60/80/100%
* ATK: +20/40/60/80/100%
* Magic ATK: +20/40/60/80/100%
* DEF: +10/20/30/40/50%
* Magic Resist: +10/20/30/40/50%
* Shop discount probability: +3/6/9/12/15%
* Battle starting SP: +1/2/3/4/5
* Refresh attempts: +1/2/3/4/5

### Notes
* In Tower of Salvation, a permanent "seed" is generated for each specifically played turn, which cannot change. What this means is, if the player has a character with a 50% Crit Rate and it attacks, missing the critical hit, the player cannot restart the turn to try to get the critical hit to occur, because the probability for that turn was defined as that character's attack not getting a critical hit.
** However, different probability seeds are applied for different attack orders. So for example, if the player switches that character's attack to happen at a different point in the turn, it has a chance to get a critical hit again.
** This also applies for any other effects with random outcomes, such as Rou/Nature's Claw's evasion.
* Differing from typical combat functionality, the player can swap characters mid-battle. It costs 2 SP to swap a character with another one.
** Swapping a character resets all of its cooldowns, so the player can negate cooldowns by replacing an unit and then adding it back, at the total cost of 4 SP.

### Seasons and rewards
Tower of Salvation runs on a seasonal schedule that lasts for 4 weeks at a time. In every season, players can obtain personal rewards as well as collaboration rewards. Personal rewards are granted based on each floor the player clears during the season. For collaboration rewards, every player contributes to the season goal by progressing through the Tower of Salvation and earing season goal points. Points are reset at the start of each season, and are capped at 15,000,000,000, which when reached, gives all players all the rewards. The player must contribute by completing at least one floor of the Tower of Salvation to receive the collaboration rewards for that season.

Attempts conclude at 9 hours before daily reset time on the day the season ends. During that time, Tower of Salvation cannot be played.

Every season resets the rewards so they can be regained again. Seasons also reset the player's starting growth and accumulated Night World Obsidian.

For the personal rewards, they are granted retroactively for all lower levels. For example, if the player starts from level 5 and clears it, they will also immedaitely gain all the rewards from levels 1 through 4. In addition to granting a resource reward, every level grants a Night World Obsidian multiplier reward which allows the player to gain more Night World Obsidian by quick battling or normally entering that floor.
