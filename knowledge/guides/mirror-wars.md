**Mirror Wars** is the first Combat Content pack and first overall content pack.

## About
:''Devoted to desires more than the Succubus and obsessed with power more than demons. Humans who burn their souls in the circle of moral destruction beautifully! For you, I, Asmode, have prepared a great stage. Prove your strength by fighting with other enemies, allies, and your other selves that came from countless different worlds! Now, shall we announce the opening of the "Mirror Wars"...!

This is a PvP (Player vs. Player) pack where players form an offensive and defensive formation and battle against other random players, climbing the ranks in the processs. Mirror Wars is unlocked after the player clears the 3rd story pack, Mist Man.

This pack was released on June 22, 2023, alongside the game's original launch. 

## Overview

Upon entrance to Mirror Wars, players can view their formation and enter battle by going through the red portal in the middle of the room, or view rankings by going to the NPC at the bar.

### Formations

When entering the formation management screen, the player is prompted to create an offensive and defensive formation. The offensive formation is used when battling against other players, and the defensive formation is what other players face when they battle against you.

By default, all gear and bonding in Mirror Wars formations is locked when the player clicks "Save", and it is not modified no matter what the player does outside of Mirror Wars. Gear and bonding can be changed by clicking "Refresh Gear Info" at the bottom of the screen, which will update them to be whatever the player currently has enabled. However, engraving, awakening, costume duplicates, potential liberation, and refining are automatically applied in Mirror Wars without needing a manual refresh.

### Battles
Mirror Wars uses Blood cocktails as a currency needed to engage in battles. Every day at daily reset time, the player is granted up to 40 Blood cocktails automatically. These cannot be banked, so if the player doesn't use all 40 in a day, they will only be refreshed back up to the maximum of 40 the next day. Blood cocktails can additionally be bought from the Charge store for 40 cocktails per 170 Dia.

All battles in Mirror Wars are automatic, meaning the player cannot change their characters' positioning or select skills once they enter battle. As such, the player needs to set the positioning and skill order for all their characters pre-emptively before entering battle.

In battle, the attacker always goes first and the defender always goes second, however, {{kw|Preemptive Action||Preemptive}} skills activate before the first turn for both the attacker and the defender. The attacker starts with 5 {{kw|SP}} and the defender starts with 6. Each turn (including the opponent's turn), both the attacker and the defender regain 6 SP. If there is not enough SP for a character to use their queued skill, they will instead perform a basic attack. If a battle stalls for 50 turns, it automatically ends in the attacker's victory.

Burst can be set in costume usage order for battling in Mirror Wars. If no costume order is set, Burst is triggered automatically according to auto-battle rules. Each turn, the maximum possible Burst is used based on available SP.

Winning battles in Mirror Wars grants victory points, both when attacking and when succesfully defending against another player's attack, while losing reduces victory points. When attacking, the player also earns Medals of the Fighting Spirit for every battle no matter if they win or lose, although more are granted for wins. Medals of the Fighting Spirit can be used to purchase products in the Mirror Wars Shop.

The "Repeat Battle" button before entering battle can be used to play through battles automatically. The player can select anywhere from 1 to 40 Blood cocktails to be consumed for a single battle, with the Medal of the Fighting Spirit rewards scaling accordingly. However, it should be noted that victory points **do not** scale accordingly, so if the player uses all 40 daily Blood cocktails on a single battle, they will only gain or lose one battle's worth of victory points.

### Seasons
Mirror Wars operates on an on-and-off season basis. Seasons last for 1 week, from Monday to Sunday, with the off-season starting 9 hours before daily reset time on Sunday (15:00 UTC) and lasting until the reset time on Monday (0:00 UTC). During the off-season, players cannot engage in battles.

### Ranking and rewards
Mirror Wars has 11 ranks from Bronze to Champion, with most ranks also being further split into I, II, and III. When the player reaches a new rank for the first time each season, they gain a Dia reward. An additional Dia reward is granted at the end of the season based on the rank that the player finished on. More Medals of the Fighting Spirit are granted for each battle depending on how high the player's rank is.

Rankings from Bronze through Dia are based on victory points (VP), while placements from Sapphire up are instead based on placement in the leaderboards.
