**Gold** is a resource.

## Usage
Gold is the game's main currency used outside of content Shops.

Gold can be used in various ways, including:

* Buying items in field shops
* Increasing the amount of slots for resources and gear in the Bag
* Refining gear score and options
* Upgrading a costume's Potential Liberation and Burst
* Engraving and awakening a character
