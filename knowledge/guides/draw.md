# 抽卡机制与保底（Draw）

> 来源：Brown Dust 2 Wiki (browndust2.miraheze.org) · 页面「Draw（抽卡与保底）」 · 采集 2026-10-03 · 英文原文（AI 阅读用，回答以中文输出）

**Drawing** is the main way to obtain costumes and Exclusive gear in *Brown Dust 2*.

The draw menu always has ongoing **banners**. Banners refer to the different drawing options where a specific costumne or Exclusive gear can be drawn for at an increased probability, or the other permanent banners. The permanent banners that are always available are the 12-Pick Selective Draw, the regular Gear Draw, as well as the Property Costume, UR Gear, and SR Gear draws which use unique draw tickets.

Players can spend Draw Tickets, Selective Draw Tickets, Property Tickets, UR Gear Tickets, SR Gear Tickets, as well as free and paid Dia to draw. Occasionally, other special tickets may be available that can be used to drawn on unique banners.

## Notes
* Costume draws can contain any drawable costumes of all star ranks. Gear draws can contain R, SR, or UR grade Exclusive gear only.
* Limited costumes and the Exclusive gear of Collaboration characters can only be drawn from their own Pick-Up Banners when they're available. They cannot be drawn during any other time.
* The general probability for all regular banners is the same. The probability is split between all eligible options for that category.
** For costumes, this is:
*** 3% chance to draw a 5-star costume
*** 14% chance to draw a 4-star costume
*** 83% chance to draw a 3-star costume
** For weapons, this is:
*** 5% chance to draw the Exclusive gear of a 5-star character (further split into 3% UR / 2% SR)
*** 28% chance to draw the Exclusive gear of a 4-star character (further split into 2.5% UR / 8.5% SR / 17% R)
*** 67% chance to draw the Exclusive gear of a 3-star character (further split into 4% UR / 16% SR / 47% R)
* Each draw generates 1 Draw Point for that banner. Draw Points are separated between all banners.
** Draw Points are automatically converted into Powder of Hope after a timed banner ends. The player can also freely exchange their Draw Points for Powder of Hope at any time during the banner's run.
** Additionally, after accumulating 200 Draw Points on a Pick-Up Banner or 1-Pick Selective Draw, the player can directly exchange their Draw Points into a copy of that costume or UR Exclusive gear.
* There is a pity system:
** If the player does not draw a 5-star costume or the UR Exclusive gear of a 5-star character within 99 attempts, they are guaranteed one in their 100th draw and the counter is reset.
** If the player does not draw a 4-star costume or an SR Exclusive gear within 9 attempts, they are guaranteed one in their 10th draw and the counter is reset.
** For gear specificially, the pity at the 10th draw has unique probabilities:
*** 5-star character SR or higher grade Exclusive gear: 7.55%
*** 4-star character SR or higher grade Exclusive gear: 32.08%
*** 3-star character SR or higher grade Exclusive gear: 60.38%

## Types of banners
### Pick-Up Banner
:''For the list of all previous Pick-Up Banners, see Draw/History.
Limited-time banners that have an increased chance to obtain a specific costume or Exclusive gear are called **Pick-Up Banners**. Draw Tickets or Dia can be used to draw on Pick-Up Banners.

For these banners, instead of the chance to draw a 5-star costume or Exclusive gear being split equally across all options, the featured costume or gear has a higher chance to be drawn. The increased chance is always **1.5%** and does not change at any point in the banner's duration.

This means, in essence, that whenever a 5-star costume or UR Exclusive gear is drawn from a Pick-Up banner, it has a 50/50 chance to be the featured costume or UR Exclusive gear, and a 50/50 chance to be any other drawable 5-star costume or UR Exclusive gear.

For all Pick-Up Banners of non-limited costumes, that costume will always enter the Powder of Hope Shop after the next bi-weekly maintenance.

### 12-Pick Selective Draw

The 12-Pick Selective Draw is a permanent banner that allows the player to choose 12 costumes to draw for. Selective Exclusive Draw Tickets can be used in addition to Draw Tickets or Dia to draw on this banner.

For this banner, the 3% chance of drawing a 5-star costume is entirely split between those 12 costumes, meaning the player is guaranteed to draw one of their chosen costumes every time they draw a 5-star one. The only exception to this is if the player doesn't draw any 5-star costumes within 99 draws and hits the 100th draw pity: The 100th draw pity can instead be any drawable costume in the game, not just one of the 12 chosen for this banner.

Since the 12-Pick Selective Draw does not have a system to exchange Draw Points for a copy of a costume, all Draw Points must be manually converted into Powder of Hope only.

Limited costumes cannot be chosen for this banner, and new costumes that still have their first banner ongoing - even if not limited - cannot be chosen until their first banner has ended.

### 1-Pick Selective Draw
A variant of this banner, the 1-Pick Selective Draw, is only available during anniversaries. As the name suggests, the 1-Pick Selective Draw allows the player to choose any costume and draw for that specifically. Like the 12-Pick, Selective Exclusive Draw Tickets can be used for 1-Pick as well.

1-Pick functions more like a Pick-Up Banner in the way that there is only an **increased** chance to get the chosen costume. Instead of being guaranteed on a normal 5-star roll, it has a 50/50 chance to be the chosen costume, and a 50/50 chance to be any other drawable costume.

Just like 12-Pick, limited costumes cannot be chosen for this banner, and new costumes that still have their first banner ongoing - even if not limited - cannot be chosen until their first banner has ended.

### 12-Pick Exclusive Gear Selective Draw
Another variant of this banner, the 12-Pick Exclusive Gear Selective Draw, is also only available during anniversaries. As the name suggests, the 12-Pick Exclusive Gear Selective allows the player to choose 12 Exclusive gear and draw for them. Like the 12-Pick, Selective Exclusive Draw Tickets can be used for 12-Pick Gear Draw as well.

For this banner, the 3% chance of drawing the UR Exclusive gear of a 5-star character is entirely split between those 12 gear, meaning the player is guaranteed to draw one of their chosen gear every time they draw a 5-star character's UR Exclusive gear. The only exception to this is if the player doesn't draw any 5-star character UR Exclusive gear within 99 draws and hits the 100th draw pity: The 100th draw pity can instead be any drawable UR Exclusive gear of a 5-star character, not just one of the 12 chosen for this banner.

Since the 12-Pick Exclusive Gear Selective Draw does not have a system to exchange Draw Points for a copy of an Exclusive gear, all Draw Points must be manually converted into Powder of Hope only.

The Exclusive gear of Collaboration characters cannot be chosen for this banner. For new characters that still have their first Exclusive gear banner ongoing - even if not limited - their gear cannot be chosen until their first banner has ended.

The Property Selective Draw is a permanent banner, but is only visible in the draw screen if the player has at least 1 Property Selective Draw Exchange Ticket in their Bag.

For the Property Selective Draw, the player can choose any of the 5 properties. Then, the player will draw any 5-star costume from that selected property.

### Gear draws

### Regular Gear Draw
The regular Gear Draw is another permanent banner. In it, the player can draw for any Exclusive gear without there being a specific increased probability for any character's gear.

### UR Exclusive Gear Guaranteed Draw
The UR Exclusive Gear Guaranteed Draw is another permanent banner that uses a special ticket, the UR Exclusive Gear Guaranteed Draw Exchange Ticket.

Since this banner only contains UR grade Exclusive gear, the probabilities are adjusted:
* 15% chance to draw the UR Exclusive gear of a 5-star character
* 35% chance to draw the UR Exclusive gear of a 4-star character
* 50% chance to draw the UR Exclusive gear of a 3-star character

### SR Exclusive Gear Guaranteed Draw
The SR Exclusive Gear Guaranteed Draw is another permanent banner that uses a special ticket, the SR Exclusive Gear Guaranteed Draw Exchange Ticket.

Since this banner only contains SR grade Exclusive gear, the probabilities are adjusted:
* 15% chance to draw the SR Exclusive gear of a 5-star character
* 35% chance to draw the SR Exclusive gear of a 4-star character
* 50% chance to draw the SR Exclusive gear of a 3-star character

## Events

Each costume's Pick-Up Banner comes with a corresponding event mission associated with it.

The first time the player tries a Pick-Up draw, they gain 1 Draw Ticket. 10 more Draw Tickets can be gained for every copy of that costume the player draws, up to 5 copies. The 6th copy instead grants 20 Ancient Crystals.

The rewards of Pick-Up event missions are limited to one claim per account. This means that if the player claimed 3 rewards from the event mission and the costume reruns later in the future, they cannot claim those 3 rewards again, but they can continue their progress for the remaining 3 rewards.
