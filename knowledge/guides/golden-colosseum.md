# 黄金竞技场（The Golden Colosseum · PvP）

**The Golden Colosseum** is the fifth Combat Content pack and eighth overall content pack.

## About
:''Those who seek power. Those who worship power. Those who crave power and are dominated by it. That is the race called demons. So, show me — this thing you call your power. Prove that you have a place in Ghenar, and that you are worthy of being under my dominion, here in this Golden Colosseum soaked with the blood of countless demons.

This is a PvP (Player vs. Player) pack where players combine costumes and blessings, and battle against other random players, climbing the ranks in the processs. The Golden Colosseum is unlocked after the player clears the 6th story pack, Pagan Tower.

This pack was released on December 4, 2025, shortly before the beginning of the game's 2.5th Anniversary.

## Overview

The Golden Colosseum features a new ruleset every two weeks. Rules that may change are the following:

* The size of the battle grid
* Squares in the grid where characters cannot be placed
* The number of costumes that can participate in battle
* The number of points that can be used on blessings
* The list of banned costumes and blessings

In the Golden Colosseum, each costume counts as a separate character. This means that multiple costumes of the same character can be in the player's formation at the same time.

Gear is not used in the Golden Colosseum. Therefore, the strength of a costume is based purely on growth elements. The following are taken into account for characters:

* Character level
* The costume's upgrade level (duplicates)
* Character engraving and awakening
* Costume potential liberation (costumes will always be bonded to themselves)
* Costume burst (the highest unlocked burst is always used automatically for a costume)

### Battle
Unlike in Mirror Wars where the player forms an offensive and defensive formation, the Golden Colsseum only uses one formation which is used for both offense and defense. When entering battle, it is randomly decided which player goes first and which goes second. All battles are automatic, meaning the player cannot change their characters' positioning once they enter battle. As such, the player needs to set the positioning for all their characters pre-emptively before entering battle.

Winning battles in the Golden Colosseum grants victory points, while losing reduces victory points. There is no definitive "defense" system in the Golden Colosseum unlike in Mirror Wars, which means the player does not lose victory points when another player defeats their formation, and battles from other players against the player's formation are not stored in the battle history. Although no rewards are granted for individual wins, the player earns Aurum Coins for winning up to 40 battles each week. Aurum Coins can be used to purchase products in The Golden Colosseum Shop.

In the Golden Colosseum, characters act on the offensive and defensive side one after another, differing from typical combat rules. Instead of one side finishing all their actions before changing to the other side, the first player's first costume triggers its skill, then the second player's first costume, then the first player's second costume, and so on. Turns progress once all characters on both sides have taken their action.

Various other changes to typical combat rules are also adjusted for the Golden Colsseum:

*  is unlimited. The player's SP is considered to be 0.
*  do not reset at the end of the turn.
* The cooldown of all skills is set to 0 turns.
*  cannot be set to trigger before battles start. Instead, these skills can only be activated normally when it is that costume's turn to act.

After 5 full turns have progressed without a player winning, Death Time is activated. In Death Time:

* Characters can only perform basic attacks
* ATK and Magic ATK are increased by 100%
* DEF and Magic Resist are reduced by 100%
* Damage taken is increased by 50%
* These effects are reapplied and stacked every 2 turns in Death Time

The player can attempt up to 100 battles each week, however attempts can be reset for 10 Dia up to 3 times each week, meaning the grand total of possible battles each week is 400. The "Repeat Battle" button before entering battle can be used to play through battles automatically.

### Blessings
Unique to the Golden Colosseum, passive effects called Gladiator's Blessings can be chosen to provide effects to the player's formation. Any blessings can be chosen as long as the player stays within their point limit. Separate blessings must be chosen for when the player goes first or second, and the point limit is usually higher for going second. The stronger a blessing is, the more points it costs.

Blessings are activated first for the player who attacks first, and they are applied in the order that they're equipped.

For the list of blessings, see #List of blessings below.

### Seasons
The Golden Colosseum operates on an on-and-off season basis. Seasons last for 1 week, from Thursday to Wednesday, with the off-season starting 9 hours before daily reset time on Wednesday (15:00 UTC) and lasting until the reset time on Thursday (0:00 UTC). During the off-season, players cannot engage in battles.

### Ranking and rewards
The Golden Colosseum has 11 ranks from Bronze to Champion, with most ranks also being further split into I, II, and III. A reward of Aurum Coins is granted at the end of the season based on the rank that the player finished on. Additionally, more Aurum Coins are granted for winning up to 40 times in The Golden Colosseum each week.

Unlike in Mirror Wars where ranking is based on a mixture of victory points and placement in the leaderboards, Golden Colsseum ranking is always based only on placement in the leaderboards.
