**Dia** is a resource.

## Usage
Dia is the game's premium currency and is further split into two categories: **Paid Dia** and **Free Dia**.

### Paid Dia
There are two main ways to use Paid Dia:

* The player can use 90 Paid Dia once per day to draw on a pick-up banner once (as opposed to the regular 200 Free Dia cost).
* For special events such as collaborations and anniversaries, certain costumes will feature "Step-Up Draws" where the player can spend up to 5000 Paid Dia to draw on that costume, eventually guaranteeing a copy of that costume.

In addition to these, other special banners or shop products may appear that can only be purchased with Paid Dia.

### Free Dia
Free Dia can be used for various purposes:

* Free Dia can be used to draw costumes or gear. 1 draw costs 200 Free Dia.
* In the Charge store, the player can buy more Blood cocktails, Cooked Rice, Torches, and Keys of Salvation.
* In the Items Shop, a Nickname Ticket or Guild Name Change Ticket can be bought for 200 Dia.
* In My Room, certain decorations can be bought for different Dia prices, ranging from 50 to 400.
* In The Golden Colosseum, the player can refresh their battle attempts for 10 Dia.
* The monthly Premium Season Pass can be bought for 1000 Dia.

## Obtaining
### Paid Dia
Paid Dia is only obtainable from paid packages for real money, such as the Monthly Dia Packages.

### Free Dia
Free Dia can be obtained from various sources:

### Recurring

** Source || Amount** 

·  Fully clear all normal battles in seasonal events  ·  750x per 2 weeks

·  Complete all Boosting Missions in seasonal events  ·  600x per 2 weeks

·  Mirror Wars end-of-season reward  ·  400x ~ 2000x per week (depending on rank)

·  Mirror Wars rank promotion reward  ·  Up to 680x per week (depending on rank)

·  Check in to the [Official Web Shop](链接)  ·  10x per day

·  Daily Dispatch reward  ·  Varies per day (random)

### One-time

** Source || Amount** 

·  Reach Achievement levels  ·  Varies by level

·  Find and open a treasure chest in a Field  ·  50x

·  Read through a Slice of Life story  ·  20x

·  Read through a Fated Guest's encounter event  ·  50x per encounter

·  Have a specific costume visit the Glupy Diner 3 times  ·  100x

·  Win battles in story, character, and event packs  ·  Varies per pack and difficulty

·  Clear a story pack in normal difficulty  ·  100x + 100x from Clear Package

·  Clear a story pack in hard difficulty  ·  150x + 100x from Clear Package

·  Clear a story pack in very hard difficulty  ·  200x + 100x from Clear Package

·  Tower of Desire reward every X0 floor (10, 20, 30, etc)  ·  150x + 100x from Clear Package

·  Tower of Desire reward every X5 floor (15, 25, 35, etc)  ·  100x

·  Tower of Wrath reward every X0 floor (10, 20, 30, etc)  ·  150x

·  Tower of Wrath reward every X5 floor (15, 25, 35, etc)  ·  100x

·  Tower of Jealousy reward every X0 floor (10, 20, 30, etc)  ·  150x

·  Tower of Jealousy reward every X5 floor (15, 25, 35, etc)  ·  100x

In addition to these, Free Dia may be granted from various other sources such as paid bundles, events, log-in rewards, or other gifts.
