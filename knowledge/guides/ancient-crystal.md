**Ancient Crystal** is a resource.

## Usage
Ancient Crystals are used for crafting UR grade gear. Each crafting attempt costs 5 Ancient Crystals. For more information on crafting, see Gear.

## Obtaining
Ancient Crystals can be obtained from various sources:

### Recurring

** Source || Amount || Price (per Crystal)** 

·  Seasonal event shop  ·  40x per 2 weeks  ·  300 Event Currency for 5x

·  Golden Thread Shop  ·  30x per calendar month  ·  10 {{Icon|Golden Thread}}

·  The Golden Colosseum Shop  ·  50x per calendar month  ·  100 {{Icon|Aurum Coin}} for 5x

·  Refinement Remnant Shop  ·  10x per calendar month  ·  6 {{Icon|Refinement Remnant}}

·  Tower of Salvation end-of-season reward  ·  30x per 4 weeks  ·  Free

·  Daily Tower of Pride reward  ·  6x ~ 9x per day (depending on score)  ·  Free

·  Daily Last Night reward  ·  1x ~ 5x per day (depending on damage)  ·  Free

### One-time

** Source || Amount || Price (per Crystal)** 

·  Clear a Tower of Pride floor  ·  10x per floor  ·  Free

·  Tower of Desire reward every 5 floors  ·  Varies  ·  Free

In addition to these, Ancient Crystals may be granted from various other sources such as paid bundles, season passes, log-in rewards, or other gifts.
